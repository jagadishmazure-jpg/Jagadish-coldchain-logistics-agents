# Temperature Breach Response Protocol

## Breach Definition
A breach is any confirmed reefer temperature reading outside the 0-4 C band
for fresh perishables (see the temperature control standard). A single
reading may be a sensor glitch; two consecutive out-of-range readings, or one
reading confirmed by a manual check, constitutes a confirmed breach.

## Immediate Actions (0-30 Minutes)
1. Quarantine the trailer: no further loading or unloading.
2. Notify QA and the dispatcher with the truck ID, timestamp, and peak
   temperature.
3. Instruct the driver to keep the reefer running and doors closed.
4. Log the incident in the audit system with the sensor readings attached.

## Assessment and Disposition (30 Minutes - 4 Hours)
QA reviews the time-temperature curve and decides one of: accept the load,
recondition (rapid chill and re-inspect), or destroy. Product held above
8 C for more than 2 hours is destroyed without exception. Every decision and
its justification is recorded; the customer is notified before any
reconditioned product ships.

## Customer Communication
The customer is informed of any confirmed breach within 4 hours, with the
readings, the QA disposition, and the corrective action. Never ship
quarantined product while assessment is pending.
